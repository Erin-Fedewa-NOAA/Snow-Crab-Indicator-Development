#INDICATOR_NAME
"Summer_Cold_Pool_SEBS_Survey"

#DESCRIPTION
"The total area (nmi²) of all EBS bottom trawl survey stations with bottom temperatures ≤ 0°C. 
                      Proposed sign of the relationship is positive. "

#STATUS_TRENDS
"The spatial extent of cold-water habitat ≤ 0°C increased from 2025 to 2026, and is
                        now slightly above the time series mean."

#FACTORS
"The spatial extent of bottom waters ≤ 0°C is driven by winter sea ice extent and winds."

#IMPLICATIONS
"The EBS snow crab population collapse was attributed to warming and sea ice loss 
                       during the 2018-2019 marine heatwave (Szuwalski et al., 2023; Litzow et al., 2024), 
                       and cold-water habitat ≤ 0°C is critical for supporting high snow crab densities, 
                       energetic condition and survival in rebuilding towards a pre-collapse state (Fedewa et al., 2025). "
