#SUBMISSION_YEAR

#INDICATOR_NAME
"Winter_Sea_Ice_Advance_BS_Satellite_ERA"

#DESCRIPTION
"March-April average winter sea ice concentration in the EBS and NBS bottom trawl survey area
                      from the ERA5 reanalysis dataset. Concentration values represent the percentage of the Bering Sea 
                      covered by sea ice, with values below 15% considered ice-free. Proposed sign of the relationship is positive"

#STATUS_TRENDS
"Sea ice extent increased substantially from spring 2025 to spring 2026, and is well  
                        above the time series mean. The spring 2026 sea ice extent estimate is the highest 
                        observed the Bering Sea since the last cold stanza that ended in 2013"

#FACTORS
"Sea ice in the Bering Sea is driven by atmospheric CO2, ocean heat transport and winds."

#IMPLICATIONS
"Low levels of sea ice are associated with dampened productivity of snow crab (Mullowney et al., 2023), 
                       so declines in sea ice concentration suggest poor conditions for recruitment."

#REFERENCES
"Mullowney DRJ, Baker KD, Szuwalski CS, Boudreau SA, Cyr F, et al. (2023) Sub-Arctic no more: Short- and long-term global-scale prospects for snow crab (Chionoecetes opilio) under global warming. PLOS Climate 2(10): e0000294. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pclm.0000294"
