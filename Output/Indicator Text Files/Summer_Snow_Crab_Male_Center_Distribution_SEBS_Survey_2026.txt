#INDICATOR_NAME
"Summer_Snow_Crab_Male_Center_Distribution_SEBS_Survey"

#DESCRIPTION
"CPUE-weighted average latitude of the mature male snow crab stock during the EBS summer bottom 
                      trawl survey. Proposed sign of the relationship is positive."

#STATUS_TRENDS
"Mature male center of abundance changed very little from 2025 to 2026, and remains
                        near the time series mean."

#FACTORS
"Historically, centroids of abundance have tracked bottom temperatures, and were further south 
                  with colder temperatures (Orensanz et al., 2005)."

#IMPLICATIONS
"Centroids of abundance are expected to shift north under warming as snow crab track preferred 
                       cold-water habitat. This may have implications for availability to the snow crab fishery and 
                       NOAA bottom trawl survey."

#REFERENCES
"Orensanz, J., Ernst, B., Armstrong, D., Stabeno, P., and Livingston, P. 2005. Contraction of the geographic range of distribution of snow crab (Chionoecetes opilio) in the Eastern Bering Sea: An environmental ratchet? Reports of California Cooperative Oceanic Fisheries Investigations, 45: 65-79."
